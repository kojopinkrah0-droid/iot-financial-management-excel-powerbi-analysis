# Power BI Dashboard Build Guide

## Page 1 — Executive Financial Overview
Cards: Total Revenue, Total Net Profit, Average ROI, Total EBITDA, Total Cash Flow, Profit Margin %.
Visuals: Revenue and Net Profit by Region; Net Profit by Department; Financial Status distribution.
Slicers: Region, Department, Financial Status, Credit Risk Level.

## Page 2 — IoT Operations & Automation
Cards: Average System Uptime, Automation Efficiency, Performance Score, Device Error Rate.
Visuals: Automation Efficiency by Department; ERP Response Time vs Performance Score; Network Latency by Region; Device Error Rate by Department.

## Page 3 — Risk & Compliance
Cards: Average Fraud Risk, Compliance Score, High Fraud Risk Transactions, Security Breach Attempts.
Visuals: Fraud Risk by Credit Risk Level; Compliance by Region; Security Breach Attempts by Department; Anomaly Score vs Fraud Risk.

## Page 4 — Financial Drivers
Visuals: ROI by Department and Region; Gross Margin vs Automation Efficiency scatter; Operating Cost vs Net Profit; Cash Flow by Financial Status.

Use conditional formatting for Financial Status (Excellent/Good/Moderate/Poor) and risk metrics. Keep Region and Department slicers synchronized across pages.
